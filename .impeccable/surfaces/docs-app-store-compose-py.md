# App Store screenshot slides

Scope: the App Store screenshots for Ithaca for Plex, six slides per device (iPhone 1320 × 2868, scaled to 1206 × 2622; iPad 13-inch 2064 × 2752; Apple TV 3840 × 2160; iPhone Duo 2853 × 2007, the inner screen in landscape) and the product page header, rendered by `docs/app-store/compose.py` from simulator captures of the demo library. Mode: Persuade.

Audience: Plex owners, many with remux libraries, choosing a player on the App Store. Belief to land: this is the native player that plays their files as made. Proof: the real app screens, captured against the "Ithaca Demo" library (Blender Studio open movies, public-domain films). Constraints: no Plex, Dolby, DTS or Apple logos; no invented claims, reviews or awards; no third-party copyrighted art; must not look like Dusk for Plex's listing (dark gradient, centred headline, device below).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each slide is a theatrical one-sheet for what Ithaca does: the device is the key art, the claim is the title, a billing block credits the features. It refuses the gradient-headline-device template every player ships, Dusk's included.

OWN-WORLD: Poster black #0B0B0D. The key art's own colour bleeds out around the device from the film on its screen, darkened and tinted toward black. White type only. Anton for the title, Six Caps for the tagline, billing and date line; billing lines fully justified with tiny two-line role labels ("PICTURE IN", "SOUND IN", "ALSO STARRING"). No decorative gradients, badges, cards or logos.

STORY: The visitor sees a film they would want to watch on a device that reads as a poster, reads the claim as its title, scans the billing for their formats, and believes Ithaca is the native Plex player that respects the file.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Tagline small and tracked at top centre; the device large on the centre axis, the film's art continuing past its frame; a thin resume line under the device, the only lit mark besides the screen; the two-line title at full width below; the billing block; the date line at the foot. Every device in the set stands on the same baseline.

FORM: The One-Sheet, position 3 of my ordered grounded list in re-roll round 1. seed:ae45502d5cb0e59a

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
